# Meeting Type Taxonomy

## Placement Meeting
Keywords: Tri-part, Placement Meeting, Placement Call, Placement

## Progress Meeting
Keywords: Progress, Progress Meeting, Progress Call

## Wellbeing Meeting
Keywords: Wellbeing, Wellbeing Meeting, Wellbeing Catch-Up, Well being, Well-being

## Fallback (unmatched events)
For meetings that aren't captured in the above keywords, make a single to do with the meeting name and the option to manually add to-dos


## Excluded (never trigger, no to-do at all - not even fallback)
Keywords: lunch, meditation, admin, out of office, busy, focus time, commuting, commute
