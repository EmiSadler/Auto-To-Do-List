Auto-To-Do-List

A Chrome extension that turns your Google Calendar into an automatic checklist.

When a meeting starts on your calendar, Auto-To-Do-List detects it, works out what kind of meeting it is, and generates the right checklist automatically - shown right in a side panel in Chrome. Built for coaching and placement work at Makers Academy.

What it does

  • Watches your Google Calendar and detects when a meeting starts
  • Classifies the meeting by keywords in its title
  • Generates a relevant to-do checklist automatically
  • Lets you fully customize meeting types, keywords, and checklists per person
  • Keeps everything local to your own browser - nothing is sent anywhere else
